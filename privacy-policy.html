Privacy Policy

Daily Meditation (Android) · Effective date: 13 November 2026

  • No account, no sign-up, no login.
  • Your practice data (sessions, streaks, goals, reminders, settings) stays on your device. We have no server and never receive it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects device data to serve and measure ads.
  • Optional tips are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete all app data at any time in Settings → Delete all my data.

1. Who we are

Daily Meditation (“the app”, “we”) is a free meditation app for Android, published on Google Play by Nguyễn Văn Linh. For any privacy question, contact us at linhart1904@gmail.com.

2. Data stored only on your device

The app works without an account. The following data is created by your use of the app and stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is funded by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in Android settings);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data.

We do not send AdMob any of the practice data listed in section 2. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Consent. In regions where it is required (for example, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. If you decline, you only see non-personalized ads and every feature keeps working. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices (shown where this option is required).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Tips (Google Play Billing)

You can optionally leave a tip. Tips unlock nothing; every feature stays free. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your name, card or other payment details. The app only receives a purchase confirmation, which it uses to finish the purchase and say thank you. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts, and no collection of your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No microphone, camera, location or health data.
  • No selling of personal data.

7. Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete all data: Settings → Delete all my data erases everything stored by the app on your device. Uninstalling the app does the same.
  • Ads: change your consent in Ad privacy choices, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the app or in Android settings.

Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. For data processed by Google, use the controls Google provides.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Security

Your practice data stays in the app’s private storage on your device, protected by Android’s app sandbox. Communication with Google’s ad and billing services uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
